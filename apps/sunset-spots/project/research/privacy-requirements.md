# דרישות למדיניות פרטיות (R8a)

נאסף ב־2026-11-25 על ידי Gemini 4 Neon high עם חיפוש Google. Grok הגיע למגבלת השימוש. המנהל קרא את התוצר. זה תוצר של מודל, לא ייעוץ משפטי: את הנוסח הסופי מאשר בעל מקצוע.

**הממצא החשוב לטופס Data safety:** לפי ההגדרה של Play, מיקום מדויק הוא שטח של פחות מ־3 קמ"ר. תא של 111 מ' (D7) הוא כ־0.012 קמ"ר, ולכן נחשב **מיקום מדויק**. גם תחזית השמיים, שמעוגלת לכ־1 קמ"ר, נחשבת מדויקת. כדי ש־Open-Meteo יקבל רק "מיקום משוער", צריך תא של כ־1.8 ק"מ ומעלה. ההחלטה בין השניים היא של המשתמש/ת (R4).

> *Note: This is research documentation, not legal advice.*

## 1. Required Privacy Policy Contents Under Google Play's User Data Policy & Data Safety

Every app on Google Play—even one that collects no personal data—must provide a privacy policy URL in Play Console (`App content`) **and** link to it within the app itself ([Google Play User Data Policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10144311); [Prepare your app for review](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859455)). Under Google Play's User Data policy, the policy **must** include ([Google Play User Data Policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10144311)):

- **Clear labeling & entity match:** Explicitly labeled as a "Privacy Policy" and naming the developer/entity matching your Google Play store listing (or the app name) ([Google Play User Data Policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10144311)).
- **Privacy contact:** Developer information and a privacy point of contact or mechanism (e.g., email) to submit inquiries ([Google Play User Data Policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10144311)).
- **Comprehensive data disclosures:** All types of personal and sensitive user data accessed, collected, used, and shared by the app (including third-party APIs/SDKs), and the parties with which data is shared—matching your **Data safety** form ([Google Play User Data Policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10144311); [Data safety](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469)).
- **Secure data handling:** How data is protected (e.g., HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit, on-device storage) ([Google Play User Data Policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10144311); [Data safety](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469)).
- **Retention and deletion policy:** How long data is retained and how users can delete it (for this app: explaining no developer-side storage, how to clear local saved spots, and referencing third-party retention) ([Google Play User Data Policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10144311)).
- **Local law compliance:** Adherence to applicable local privacy laws in jurisdictions where the app is offered ([Google Play User Data Policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10144311)).
- **Not applicable here:** Account-deletion flow (no accounts). **UNCERTAIN** if background location is ever added (background location would additionally require prominent in-app disclosure and runtime consent) ([Google Play User Data Policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10144311)).

## 2. Google Play Data Safety Form Mapping for These Flows

Google Play defines **Collected** as transmitting data off the user's device, **Shared** as transferring collected data to a third party (excluding **service providers** acting solely on your behalf, legal obligations, anonymized data, or explicit user-initiated transfers), **Approximate location** as an area **≥ 3 km²**, and **Precise location** as an area **< 3 km²** ([Data safety](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469)).

| Flow | Recommended Data Safety Answer | Rationale & Caveats |
|---|---|---|
| **Device GPS/location (on-device only)** | **Not collected / Not shared** (do not declare) | On-device-only processing that never leaves the device is exempt from "Collected" ([Data safety](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469)). |
| **Saved places snapped to ~111 m grid → Open-Meteo** | **UNCERTAIN** — conservatively declare **Location → Precise location**, **Collected** (purpose: *App functionality*), and evaluate **Processed ephemerally** / **Shared** | • A 111 m × 111 m cell is **~0.012 km² (< 3 km²)**, so by area it falls under **Precise location**, not Approximate ([Data safety](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469)).<br>• **UNCERTAIN** whether user-saved viewpoints count as "user or device physical location" if unrelated to current GPS; however, saved places can reveal frequently visited/home areas.<br>• **Processed ephemerally:** Off-device data processed only in memory for a real-time request must still be entered in the Play Console form (checking "processed ephemerally"), which suppresses it from the public store label ([Data safety](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469)). **UNCERTAIN** if Open-Meteo qualifies as strictly ephemeral or a "service provider": Open-Meteo uses no cookies/tracking, but its terms reserve the right to monitor/block IPs for rate-limiting/misuse ([Open-Meteo Terms](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms)), and there is no custom service-provider contract. |
| **OpenStreetMap tiles & Overpass queries (IP + viewed area)** | **UNCERTAIN** — conservatively consider **Location → Approximate location** (for IP-inferred location), **Collected** (and potentially **Shared**), purpose: *App functionality* | • OSMF tile servers log IP addresses and request details across global cache servers for operations/anti-abuse ([OSMF Privacy Policy](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy)); public Overpass servers also see the IP and queried bounding box.<br>• Google Play requires declaring IP addresses under **Approximate location** when used to infer location ([Data safety](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469)). **UNCERTAIN** whether incidental HTTP IPs sent directly to public third-party APIs (without an SDK) solely for tiles/queries trigger "Collected"/"Shared" in Play Console review, since OSM/Overpass operate under their own policies rather than solely as your "service provider" ([Data safety](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469); [OSMF Privacy Policy](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy)). |
| **Optional GoatCounter analytics (if added later)** | **App activity → App interactions** (+ **Location → Approximate location** if country/region lookup is enabled), **Collected** (purpose: *Analytics*), **Not shared** | • GoatCounter uses no cookies/`localStorage` and does not store raw IPs, hashing IP + User-Agent with a daily salt and optionally deriving country/region ([GoatCounter Privacy](https://goatcounter.com/help/privacy)).<br>• Because it acts solely on your behalf without cross-app profiling, it fits the **service provider** exemption (not "Shared") ([Data safety](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469)). Must be declared if used in the app or a WebView/TWA you control ([Data safety](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469)). |
| **Security & Deletion** | **Encrypted in transit: Yes** (only if 100% HTTPS); **No accounts** | Answer "Yes" to encryption in transit **only** if every endpoint (Open-Meteo, OSM, Overpass, GoatCounter) uses HTTPS ([Data safety](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469)). Declaring non-ephemeral collected data without a deletion request/auto-deletion mechanism displays "Data can't be deleted" ([Data safety](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469)). |

## 3. Israel Privacy Protection Law (PPL) with Amendment 13 (In Force August 14, 2025)

Amendment 13 overhauled the Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981, introducing strong Privacy Protection Authority (PPA) administrative fines and expanded statutory damages ([PPA Amendment 13 Hub](https://govextra.gov.il/privacy/privacy-protection-authority/amendment-13/); [PPA Portal](https://www.gov.il/en/departments/privacy_protection_authority)). For a small individual developer running this free app:

- **Database registration:** **Not required.** Amendment 13 abolished general private-sector database registration; registration is now required only for public bodies and data brokers / direct-mailing databases collecting personal data to transfer to others as a business/for consideration covering **>10,000 individuals** ([PPA Amendment 13 Hub](https://govextra.gov.il/privacy/privacy-protection-authority/amendment-13/)). A separate **notification** (not registration) to the PPA is required only for databases holding **data of special sensitivity** on **>100,000 individuals** ([PPA Amendment 13 Hub](https://govextra.gov.il/privacy/privacy-protection-authority/amendment-13/)).
- **Data Protection Officer (DPO):** **Not required.** Mandatory only for public bodies, data brokers (>10,000 individuals), entities whose core activity is regular and systematic monitoring of individuals on a large scale, or entities whose core activity is large-scale processing of data of special sensitivity ([PPA Amendment 13 Hub](https://govextra.gov.il/privacy/privacy-protection-authority/amendment-13/)).
- **Section 11 notice duties:** Section 11 applies whenever requesting personal information from an individual for storage or use in a database ([PPA Amendment 13 Hub](https://govextra.gov.il/privacy/privacy-protection-authority/amendment-13/); [PPA Portal](https://www.gov.il/en/departments/privacy_protection_authority)).
  - **Applicability to a server-less developer is UNCERTAIN:** Because you operate no backend database of your own (only local device storage plus client-side requests to third-party servers, or later aggregated GoatCounter stats), it is **UNCERTAIN** whether Israeli enforcement would treat you as a "database controller" triggering Section 11. Because Amendment 13 carries administrative fines for defective notices ([PPA Amendment 13 Hub](https://govextra.gov.il/privacy/privacy-protection-authority/amendment-13/)), including the 5 Section 11 items in your privacy policy is strongly recommended:
    1. Whether providing data is legally mandatory or voluntary (consent-based), **and the consequences of refusal** (tightened under Amendment 13) ([PPA Amendment 13 Hub](https://govextra.gov.il/privacy/privacy-protection-authority/amendment-13/)).
    2. Purpose(s) of collection/processing ([PPA Amendment 13 Hub](https://govextra.gov.il/privacy/privacy-protection-authority/amendment-13/)).
    3. Identity and contact details of the database controller ([PPA Amendment 13 Hub](https://govextra.gov.il/privacy/privacy-protection-authority/amendment-13/)).
    4. Recipients / categories of recipients to whom data is transferred and the purpose of transfer ([PPA Amendment 13 Hub](https://govextra.gov.il/privacy/privacy-protection-authority/amendment-13/)).
    5. Existence of rights to inspect/access data (Section 13) and request correction or deletion of inaccurate/outdated data (Section 14) ([PPA Amendment 13 Hub](https://govextra.gov.il/privacy/privacy-protection-authority/amendment-13/); [PPA Portal](https://www.gov.il/en/departments/privacy_protection_authority)).

## 4. GDPR Article 13 Minimum Notice Items (If EU Users Can Install It)

**Territorial scope threshold (Art. 3(2)):** Under EDPB Guidelines 3/2018, mere technical accessibility from the EU does **not** trigger GDPR; there must be an intention to target/offer services to individuals in the EU (e.g., EU storefronts, EU languages/marketing) or behavioral monitoring ([EDPB Guidelines 3/2018](https://edpb.europa.eu/our-work-tools/our-documents/guidelines/guidelines-32018-territorial-scope-gdpr-article-3_en)). Whether publishing a free English app globally via Play Store defaults alone counts as targeting is **UNCERTAIN**; if EU users are served/targeted, provide all **Article 13** disclosures at or before collection ([EUR-Lex GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj); [GDPR Art. 13](https://gdpr-info.eu/art-13-gdpr/)):

1. **Art. 13(1)(a):** Controller identity and contact details (your name/contact email), plus EU representative if applicable ([GDPR Art. 13](https://gdpr-info.eu/art-13-gdpr/)). *(Under [GDPR Art. 27](https://gdpr-info.eu/art-27-gdpr/), non-EU controllers are exempt from appointing an EU representative if processing is occasional, excludes large-scale special categories, and is unlikely to result in risk—whether a continuously available public app/PWA qualifies as "occasional" is **UNCERTAIN**.)*
2. **Art. 13(1)(b):** DPO contact details, where applicable *(not applicable here)* ([GDPR Art. 13](https://gdpr-info.eu/art-13-gdpr/)).
3. **Art. 13(1)(c):** Processing purposes and **legal basis** (e.g., Art. 6(1)(b) providing requested forecast/map features, Art. 6(1)(f) legitimate interests for security/cookieless analytics, Art. 6(1)(a) consent for optional location permission) ([GDPR Art. 13](https://gdpr-info.eu/art-13-gdpr/)).
4. **Art. 13(1)(d):** The legitimate interests pursued, whenever relying on Art. 6(1)(f) ([GDPR Art. 13](https://gdpr-info.eu/art-13-gdpr/)).
5. **Art. 13(1)(e):** Recipients or categories of recipients (Open-Meteo, OSM tile servers, Overpass host, GitHub Pages for PWA, GoatCounter if added) ([GDPR Art. 13](https://gdpr-info.eu/art-13-gdpr/)).
6. **Art. 13(1)(f):** Third-country transfers and adequacy decision / safeguards ([GDPR Art. 13](https://gdpr-info.eu/art-13-gdpr/)). Note: the European Commission reaffirmed **Israel's adequacy decision** on January 15, 2024 ([EC Report COM(2024) 7 final](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A52024DC0007)); also note US/global CDN transfers for GitHub Pages and OSM tile caches ([About GitHub Pages](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/about-github-pages); [OSMF Privacy Policy](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy)).
7. **Art. 13(2)(a):** Storage period or criteria used to determine it ([GDPR Art. 13](https://gdpr-info.eu/art-13-gdpr/)).
8. **Art. 13(2)(b):** Rights to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and data portability ([GDPR Art. 13](https://gdpr-info.eu/art-13-gdpr/)).
9. **Art. 13(2)(c):** Right to withdraw consent at any time (where processing relies on consent, e.g., revoking location permission) ([GDPR Art. 13](https://gdpr-info.eu/art-13-gdpr/)).
10. **Art. 13(2)(d):** Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority ([GDPR Art. 13](https://gdpr-info.eu/art-13-gdpr/)).
11. **Art. 13(2)(e):** Whether providing data is a statutory/contractual requirement, whether obliged to provide it, and consequences of refusal ([GDPR Art. 13](https://gdpr-info.eu/art-13-gdpr/)).
12. **Art. 13(2)(f):** Existence of automated decision-making/profiling under Art. 22 *(state none)* ([GDPR Art. 13](https://gdpr-info.eu/art-13-gdpr/)).
13. **Art. 13(3):** Prior notice before repurposing data for a new purpose (e.g., before enabling GoatCounter) ([GDPR Art. 13](https://gdpr-info.eu/art-13-gdpr/)).

## 5. Policy URL Requirements & PWA-Specific Considerations

- **Public, non-editable, non-PDF URL (Required):** Yes. Google Play requires the privacy policy to be hosted on an **active, publicly accessible, non-geofenced URL** (accessible without login), **non-editable** (not an open Google Doc or editable wiki), and **no PDFs** or downloadable files ([Google Play User Data Policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10144311); [Prepare your app for review](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859455)). A static HTTPS HTML page on GitHub Pages (e.g., `https://<user>.github.io/<repo>/privacy.html`) satisfies this—do not link to a `.pdf` or an editable repository view ([Google Play User Data Policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10144311); [About GitHub Pages](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/about-github-pages)).
- **PWA-specific points:**
  - **GitHub Pages IP logging:** GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes on GitHub Pages sites regardless of login state ([About GitHub Pages](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/about-github-pages)); disclose GitHub Pages as host/recipient for the PWA.
  - **Local storage & Service Worker cache (ePrivacy Art. 5(3)):** EDPB Guidelines 2/2023 confirm Art. 5(3) is technology-neutral and covers `localStorage`, `IndexedDB`, and Service Worker caches ([EDPB Guidelines 2/2023](https://edpb.europa.eu/our-work-tools/our-documents/guidelines/guidelines-22023-technical-scope-art-53-eprivacy-directive_en)). Storing user-saved spots and caching app-shell assets for offline PWA use falls under the **"strictly necessary"** exemption (no consent banner needed), provided it is not used for tracking ([EDPB Guidelines 2/2023](https://edpb.europa.eu/our-work-tools/our-documents/guidelines/guidelines-22023-technical-scope-art-53-eprivacy-directive_en)).
  - **GoatCounter on PWA:** Uses no cookies or `localStorage` and discards raw IPs ([GoatCounter Privacy](https://goatcounter.com/help/privacy)), avoiding ePrivacy terminal-storage consent while still warranting privacy-policy disclosure under GDPR Art. 13 ([GDPR Art. 13](https://gdpr-info.eu/art-13-gdpr/)).
  - **UI link & TWA/WebView:** Link the policy visibly in the PWA UI (e.g., About/Settings/Footer) ([GDPR Art. 13](https://gdpr-info.eu/art-13-gdpr/)). If the Android app wraps the PWA via a WebView or Trusted Web Activity (TWA) you control, Google Play's Data safety rules apply to the PWA's network flows too ([Data safety](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469)).

## Checklist of Required Privacy Policy Sections

- [ ] **Title & Effective Date:** Clearly titled "Privacy Policy," naming the app and the developer matching your Google Play listing ([User Data Policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10144311)).
- [ ] **Controller / Developer Identity & Contact:** Your name (or Play Console developer identity), location (Israel), and a working privacy contact email ([User Data Policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10144311); [GDPR Art. 13](https://gdpr-info.eu/art-13-gdpr/); [PPA Amendment 13 Hub](https://govextra.gov.il/privacy/privacy-protection-authority/amendment-13/)).
- [ ] **Data Handled & Flows (Android + PWA):**
  - [ ] Device location: used on-device only; never sent to developer servers ([Data safety](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469)).
  - [ ] Saved places: stored locally on device (`localStorage`/IndexedDB/Android storage); snapped (~111 m) coordinates sent over HTTPS to **Open-Meteo** for elevation/weather forecasts ([Open-Meteo Terms](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms)).
  - [ ] Map tiles & queries: requests sent to **OpenStreetMap** tile servers and **Overpass** API (they receive IP address and viewed map area) ([OSMF Privacy Policy](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy)).
  - [ ] PWA hosting: **GitHub Pages** logs visitor IP addresses for security ([About GitHub Pages](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/about-github-pages)).
  - [ ] Local storage / Service Worker caching: strictly necessary for offline/saved-spots functionality; no cookies, no ads, no accounts ([EDPB Guidelines 2/2023](https://edpb.europa.eu/our-work-tools/our-documents/guidelines/guidelines-22023-technical-scope-art-53-eprivacy-directive_en)).
  - [ ] *(When/if added)* **GoatCounter**: cookieless aggregated analytics, daily salted hash, no raw IP storage ([GoatCounter Privacy](https://goatcounter.com/help/privacy)).
- [ ] **Purposes & Legal Bases (GDPR Art. 13(1)(c)–(d)):** Map each flow to its purpose and legal basis (consent for OS location; performance of requested feature / legitimate interests for API/hosting logs) ([GDPR Art. 13](https://gdpr-info.eu/art-13-gdpr/)).
- [ ] **Voluntariness & Consequences of Refusal (Israel Sec. 11 & GDPR Art. 13(2)(e)):** State providing data is voluntary (no legal obligation) and explain what stops working if declined (e.g., no GPS centering; no forecasts/maps without network requests) ([PPA Amendment 13 Hub](https://govextra.gov.il/privacy/privacy-protection-authority/amendment-13/); [GDPR Art. 13](https://gdpr-info.eu/art-13-gdpr/)).
- [ ] **Third-Party Recipients & International Transfers:** List Open-Meteo, OSMF, Overpass operator, GitHub Pages (and GoatCounter when added) with links to their policies, plus note international transfers and Israel's EU adequacy decision ([GDPR Art. 13](https://gdpr-info.eu/art-13-gdpr/); [EC Report COM(2024) 7 final](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A52024DC0007)).
- [ ] **Security, Retention & Deletion:** Confirm HTTPS in transit, state you store no user data on your own servers, explain how users delete local data (clear app/browser data or delete saved spots), and link to third-party log retention ([User Data Policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10144311); [GDPR Art. 13](https://gdpr-info.eu/art-13-gdpr/)).
- [ ] **User Rights (Israel Sec. 13–14 & GDPR Art. 13(2)(b)–(d)):** Right to access, rectify, delete/erase, restrict, object, port data, withdraw consent (revoke OS location permission), and lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Israel PPA / EU DPA) ([PPA Amendment 13 Hub](https://govextra.gov.il/privacy/privacy-protection-authority/amendment-13/); [GDPR Art. 13](https://gdpr-info.eu/art-13-gdpr/)).
- [ ] **Automated Decision-Making & Policy Changes:** State no automated decision-making/profiling (GDPR Art. 22) and how updates will be communicated before new processing (e.g., before enabling GoatCounter) ([GDPR Art. 13](https://gdpr-info.eu/art-13-gdpr/)).
- [ ] **Hosting & Placement:** Hosted as static HTML on an active, public, non-geofenced, non-editable, non-PDF HTTPS URL; linked in Google Play Console, inside the Android app, and inside the PWA ([User Data Policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10144311); [Prepare your app for review](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859455)).
